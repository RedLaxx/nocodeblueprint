Privacy policy

This policy explains what NoCode Blueprint may collect, why it is used, and what to review before enabling advertising or analytics on the site.

Effective date: October 9, 2026. This is a plain-language publication policy, not legal advice. Review it with the requirements that apply to your business, audience, and final hosting setup before launch.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the NoCode Blueprint website and its article, legal, and contact pages. The site is operated as an independent publication. For privacy questions, contact hello@nocodeblueprint.com.

Information the site may receive

  • Technical information: your hosting provider may process IP address, browser, device, request time, and error information in server logs for security, reliability, and abuse prevention.
  • Messages you send: if you email us, we receive the address, message, attachments, and any other information you choose to include. We use it to respond, handle corrections, or process a privacy request.
  • Advertising information: if advertising is enabled, Google and other approved advertising partners may use cookies, identifiers, or similar technologies to serve, measure, or personalize ads as permitted by law and your choices.

Advertising and Google AdSense

When Google AdSense is enabled, Google may use cookies to show ads based on a user's visits to this and other websites. Where required, the site will ask for consent before using non-essential advertising cookies or personalized advertising. You can learn about Google's advertising settings at Google My Ad Center and about how Google uses information at Google's partner sites page.

We will not add an advertising script until the publisher account, consent setup, and disclosures are ready. The live configuration may use additional vendors; their names and purposes should be added here before launch.

How information is used

We may use information to operate and secure the site, answer messages, publish corrections, understand aggregate site performance if analytics are enabled, comply with law, and prevent abuse. We do not sell the contents of your email to data brokers.

Cookies and choices

The site can be read without creating an account. Essential hosting functions may use technical storage or logs. Advertising, analytics, and other optional technologies should be controlled through the consent choices presented in the live site. See the Cookie policy for more detail.

Retention and security

We keep messages and records only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose they were collected, legal obligations, dispute handling, or publication history. No internet transmission is guaranteed to be perfectly secure. Do not send passwords, payment details, or confidential material by email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information. Email hello@nocodeblueprint.com with a clear request and enough context to locate it. We may need to verify the request and may retain a minimal record of how it was resolved.

Children and changes

This publication is intended for adults who are evaluating work tools. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the site, vendors, or law changes; the effective date above will change when the update is material.
