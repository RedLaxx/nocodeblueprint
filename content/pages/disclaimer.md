## AI output needs review

AI systems can be wrong, incomplete, biased, or confidently misleading. Do not use an AI output as the sole basis for a decision involving money, access, employment, eligibility, safety, legal obligations, privacy, or an external promise. Build a human review path and test with representative examples.
