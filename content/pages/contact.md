## What to include

:::checklist
- The URL of the relevant page.
- A short description of the issue or idea.
- For corrections, the text that should change and why.
- For accessibility feedback, the device or browser if it helps reproduce the problem.
:::

## Response expectations

NoCode Blueprint is an independent publication, not an emergency, financial, legal, or technical support service. We aim to review thoughtful messages, but we cannot promise a particular response time or that every pitch will receive coverage.
