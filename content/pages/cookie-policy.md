## What cookies are

Cookies are small files or similar storage technologies placed on a browser. They can help a website remember a setting, measure use, or deliver advertising. A page can also be requested with information in a server log without placing a cookie.

## Categories

- **Essential:** technology needed for security, delivery, accessibility, or a feature you explicitly request. These generally cannot be switched off through an optional consent banner.
- **Measurement:** optional analytics that help us understand aggregate visits, popular pages, and errors. No analytics should be enabled until the live vendor and consent basis are documented.
- **Advertising:** optional technologies used by Google AdSense or other approved partners to serve or measure ads. These may support personalized or contextual advertising depending on consent and settings.

## Advertising choices

When required, the site will display a consent choice before optional advertising technologies run. You can also manage Google's advertising choices through <a href="https://myadcenter.google.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google My Ad Center</a>. Browser settings can block or delete cookies, although some site functions may change.

## Third-party links

Links to Google, tools, or other websites are governed by those sites' own policies. We do not control the cookies or tracking those sites may use after you leave NoCode Blueprint.
